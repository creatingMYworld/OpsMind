About

Built for the person on call

Cloud consoles show you everything. OpsMind shows you what needs fixing first.

Cloud platforms already collect the logs and metrics. Finding the one failure that matters inside them is still manual work: filtering, cross-checking, guessing.

OpsMind does that work continuously. It groups related failures into incidents, links them to the service and resource involved, and shows the likely cause and the next step.

It runs with read-only access and never changes anything in your project.
